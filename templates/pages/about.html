I am a software engineer who cares about the parts of software that only show up months later: the shape of a module, the failure mode of a deployment, the sentence in a runbook that saves somebody an hour at 3am.

How I work

I like small teams, written decisions and boring technology. Most of my engagements start with the same question — what is actually going to break here? — and end with a system that a new engineer can reason about without me in the room.

What I do

Backend architecture, Rust and Go services, data-intensive platforms, and the developer experience work that makes the rest of it bearable. I also write about all of it here.
